Help and information about the Essenciel application.

About

Essenciel helps you find fuel stations across France, compare fuel prices, and quickly locate the best nearby options based on your preferences.

Need help?

If you encounter a problem, have a question, or would like to suggest an improvement, feel free to contact us.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are fuel prices updated in real time?

Fuel prices come from publicly available data sources and are displayed together with their latest update date.

Why are some prices outdated?

The update frequency depends on the information provided by each fuel station.
