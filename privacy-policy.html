Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all iOS applications published by SOREHA (independent developer).

Data collection

The applications do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information to external servers.

Local data

Some applications may store preferences or settings locally on the device using Apple-provided storage mechanisms (e.g. UserDefaults, Keychain). This data remains on the device only.

Third-party services

Applications may use Apple frameworks or optional third-party SDKs. These services are only used when explicitly required by app functionality.

Analytics

No tracking or advertising identifiers are used unless explicitly stated in the application description on the App Store.

Contact
